How the automatic rotation works

  1. Hosting dates are pre-assigned every 3 months.
  2. Default day is the first Sunday of that month.
  3. Hosts may switch that weekend to Saturday or Sunday.
  4. Hosts may move the date one week earlier or later.
  5. For emergencies, hosts may swap dates with another family member.
  6. If they pass, they go to the pass queue; after hosting they move to the bottom.
