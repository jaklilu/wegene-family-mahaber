How the automatic rotation works

  1. Hosting dates are pre-assigned every 3 months.
  2. Default day is Sunday; hosts may optionally change it to Saturday.
  3. Hosts may move the date one week earlier or later.
  4. For emergencies, hosts may swap dates with another family member.
